# Proposal: fix-service-content-gaps

## Why

A repo-wide audit (2026-09-28) found that parts of the published site are dead ends: 17 internal link instances across 8 files point at 6 routes that do not exist (users hit 404s from the `/services/*` category pages and several `/service-details/*` stubs), the homepage "Latest Updates" section is hardcoded to November 2025 items instead of reading the maintained `data/news.json`, and verified datasets sit unused — `data/dpwh-projects.json` (4 DPWH projects) is generated by the pipeline but rendered on no page, and 3 canonical province budget-review documents (FY2023–FY2025) exist in `data/civic/records.json` but appear in no compatibility JSON or page despite the budget page showing a "recent years pending" gap.

## What Changes

- Repoint 17 broken internal link instances to real routes:
  - `/services/social-services` cards → `/service-details/mswdo-services` (the administering office page)
  - `/services/certificates` cards → `/government` (Barangay Hall services) and `/contact` (PNP station)
  - `/services/education` card → `/service-details/mswdo-services`
  - `/services/public-safety` cards → `/contact` and `/disaster-preparedness`
  - `/service-details/general-services` breadcrumb + back-link → `/government` (was `/services/government`)
  - 4 `/service-details/*` back-links → `/services` (was `/services/services`)
  - Repointed cards gain an "Office" line sourced from the published `services.json` hand directory so the new target is self-explanatory. No new near-empty detail pages are created and no service facts are invented.
- Fill the 2 filler stub pages (`human-resource-management`, `municipal-civil-registrar`) with their published descriptions from `services.json` instead of empty descriptions and "Service details for X." body text.
- Wire the homepage "Latest Updates" section to `public/data/news.json` (latest 3 current items, same fetch/badge patterns as `/news`), replacing the 3 hardcoded i18n cards.
- Surface `data/dpwh-projects.json` on the `/budget` City Projects section as a "DPWH national infrastructure projects (reported)" block, with the file's `_status: unverified` caveat rendered; add the `src/data/` mirror required for the static import (byte-identical to the generated `data/` file).
- Surface the 3 province annual-budget review documents (Resolutions 287-2023, 375-2024, 304-2025, linking to pangasinan.gov.ph PDFs) on `/budget` below the "FY2017–2025 pending verification" card, hand-curated into the manual `transparency-docs.json` with provenance referencing the canonical `transparency-doc-province-appropriation-*` records.
- Accepted states (documented, not changed): `/legislative/resolution-framework` keeps its honest empty state (0 verified resolutions); the remaining one-paragraph `/service-details/*` pages stay as-is until per-service facts are officially verified; the collector roadmap in `docs/data-pipeline.md` is unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `internal-link-integrity`: Every internal link published by pages or data-driven JSON resolves to a real route; data-driven card links are checked as part of change review so service-category pages can never link to detail pages that were never generated.

### Modified Capabilities

(none — the DPWH and province-document surfacing follows the existing `civic-data-surfacing` requirements on unverified-data labeling and single canonical sources; homepage news wiring brings the page into compliance with the existing "no duplicated hardcoded series" requirement.)

## Impact

- Code: `src/app/services/{social-services,certificates,education,public-safety}/page.tsx`, `src/app/service-details/{general-services,human-resource-management,mswdo,municipal-civil-registrar,municipal-general-services}/page.tsx`, `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/budget/page.tsx`.
- Data: `src/data/dpwh-projects.json` (new byte-identical mirror of the generated `data/` file), `data/transparency-docs.json` + `public/data/transparency-docs.json` mirror (manual file; new `province_budget_reviews` key).
- No pipeline script changes, no dependency changes, no new routes.
- Verification: `tsc --noEmit`, production `next build`, `bun run data:generate` + `bun run verify` (regeneration must leave canonical outputs unchanged), link audit re-run.
