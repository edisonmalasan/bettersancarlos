# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Agriculture and Fisheries

## Scope

Agricultural identity, mango and bamboo industries, other farm activities, and related city offices (agriculture, veterinary, cooperatives) for San Carlos City, Pangasinan. Statistics are dated (2008-era) and need current DA/PSA updates.

## Summary

San Carlos is the "Mango and Bamboo Capital" trading center with 2008-era tree counts from archived LGU pages; city agriculture, veterinary, and cooperative offices are documented from the 2024 archived directory, while current production volumes await DA/PSA statistics.

## Findings

### Agricultural Identity

- San Carlos City is the provincial and national reference point for **carabao mango** and **bamboo products** — the "Mango and Bamboo Capital of the Philippines"/"Mango-Bamboo Capital of the North".
- The city is a **major trading center of bamboo-based products/furniture** and a **top producer of carabao mangos** and other mango varieties. | `S1`, `S3`

### Mango Industry

- **2008 report:** City had over **127,000 fruit-bearing mango trees** (LGU tourism page).
- Famous for **carabao mango** and other cultivars; products include mango pie (the city set the "world's largest mango pie" record in 2011). See `tourism/26-09-festivals.md` and `culture-history/26-09-history.md`. | `S2`

### Bamboo Industry

- Products include **nipa huts, hand-woven baskets, furniture, cabinets, lamp shades**, and related bamboo craft.
- The Mango-Bamboo Festival doubles as a trade hub for producers and traders of bamboo and mango products (see festivals file). | `S2`

### Other Agricultural Activities

- **Crop production** (including rice-growing areas historically), **livestock raising**, **inland fishing** (city is landlocked; no marine fisheries), **pottery**, and **food processing**. | `S6`

### City Agriculture Office

- **Office:** City Agriculture Office — contact (historical, 2017): (075) 955-5826.
- **City Agriculturist (2024 archived LGU directory):** Nerissa T. Cabuay (see `government/26-09-lgu-structure.md`). | `S4`, `S5`

### Animal Industry / Veterinary

- **City Veterinarian (2024 archived LGU directory):** Dr. Solomon T. Gabuyan (see `government/26-09-lgu-structure.md`).
- The old city news site documents the city's **ASF (African Swine Fever) disease control program** ("Babay ASF") in 2023 — indicating active livestock disease-control programming. | `S5`, `S7`

### Cooperatives

- City runs a **City Cooperative, Entrepreneurship, Livelihood Development Office** (2024 archived LGU directory; officer: Arlaine M. De Vera).
- A **City Cooperative Office** existed in 2017 (telephone (075) 955-5852). | `S5`, `S4`

## Verification & Uncertainty

- Tree counts and production statistics are 2008-era; current DA/PSA figures pending.
- 2017 contact numbers are historical.
- "Mango and Bamboo Capital of the Philippines" vs "of the North" are variant titles from different sources, both retained as published.
