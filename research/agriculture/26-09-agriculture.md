# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Agriculture and Fisheries

**Category:** agriculture
**Verification status:** Partially Verified — mango/bamboo data and office details from official (archived) LGU and provincial sources; statistics are dated (2008 figures) and need current DA/PSA updates.
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Agricultural Identity

- San Carlos City is the provincial and national reference point for **carabao mango** and **bamboo products** — the "Mango and Bamboo Capital of the Philippines"/"Mango-Bamboo Capital of the North".
- The city is a **major trading center of bamboo-based products/furniture** and a **top producer of carabao mangos** and other mango varieties.
- Sources: [Province of Pangasinan](https://www.pangasinan.gov.ph/city-municipalities/san-carlos-city/); [Old official site — Tourism (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170326022209/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/tourism/)

## Mango Industry

- **2008 report:** City had over **127,000 fruit-bearing mango trees** (LGU tourism page).
- Famous for **carabao mango** and other cultivars; products include mango pie (the city set the "world's largest mango pie" record in 2011). See `tourism/26-09-festivals.md` and `culture-history/26-09-history.md`.
- Source: [LGU Tourism page (archived 2024)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240603225004/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/tourism)

## Bamboo Industry

- Products include **nipa huts, hand-woven baskets, furniture, cabinets, lamp shades**, and related bamboo craft.
- The Mango-Bamboo Festival doubles as a trade hub for producers and traders of bamboo and mango products (see festivals file).
- Source: [LGU Tourism page (archived 2024)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240603225004/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/tourism)

## Other Agricultural Activities

- **Crop production** (including rice-growing areas historically), **livestock raising**, **inland fishing** (city is landlocked; no marine fisheries), **pottery**, and **food processing**.
- Source: [Wikipedia — Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos,_Pangasinan)
