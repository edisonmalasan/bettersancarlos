# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Geography

**Category:** city-profile
**Verification status:** Verified (multi-source, official + authoritative)
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Location

- San Carlos City is a **landlocked component city** in the coastal province of Pangasinan, **situated in the center of the province** ("Heart of Pangasinan").
- Area (per PSA 2013): **169.03 km²** = 3.10% of Pangasinan's total area.
- Sources: [PhilAtlas](https://www.philatlas.com/luzon/r01/pangasinan/san-carlos.html); [Old official site — Facts and Figures (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170708092007/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/facts-and-figures/)

## Coordinates & Elevation

- City center: **15.9277° N, 120.3478° E** (approx. 15°56′N, 120°21′E).
- Estimated elevation: **10.8 m** (35.4 ft) above mean sea level (PhilAtlas); Wikidata lists 12 m.
- Sources: [PhilAtlas](https://www.philatlas.com/luzon/r01/pangasinan/san-carlos.html); [Wikidata Q43165](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q43165)

## Rivers and Water

- **Agno River** — major river of Pangasinan; the city's early poblacion sites were on the western/eastern banks of the Agno (historical). Wikipedia categorizes the city as a "Populated place on the Agno River".
- **San Juan River** — historically significant (site of poblacion transfer in 1718; legend of the church bell lost in the river).
- The city is **landlocked** (no marine waterbodies).

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos,_Pangasinan); [PhilAtlas](https://www.philatlas.com/luzon/r01/pangasinan/san-carlos.html); [LGU History (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240603224959/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/history); [Old official site — History (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20171017022422/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/history/)

## Climate

Tropical climate with warm temperatures year-round and a pronounced wet season (May–October) and drier season (November–April). The Wikipedia weather box (source: World Weather Online) shows:

- Mean daily max: 30–33 °C; mean daily min: 21–24 °C.
- Wettest months: July–August (approx. 680–733 mm monthly precipitation in the cited data).
- Driest months: January (approx. 5 mm in the cited data).

**Verification note:** Figures above are from the Wikipedia-sourced World Weather Online data (accessed 2015) and should be treated as indicative. The official Philippine Atmospheric classification (Coronas type) for the city is **not yet confirmed** by an official PAGASA source in this research.

Sources: [Wikipedia — Climate section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos,_Pangasinan); [World Weather Online (via Wikipedia)](https://www.worldweatheronline.com/San-Carlos-weather-averages/Pangasinan/PH.aspx)
