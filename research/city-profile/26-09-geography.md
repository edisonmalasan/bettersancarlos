# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Geography

## Scope

Physical geography of San Carlos City, Pangasinan: location, coordinates and elevation, boundaries, distances, rivers and water, climate, and the urban/rural barangay split.

## Summary

Landlocked component city at the center of Pangasinan ("Heart of Pangasinan"), 169.03 km² per PSA 2013, center approx. 15.9277° N, 120.3478° E. Two minor conflicts are documented below (land area, elevation).

## Findings

### Location

- San Carlos City is a **landlocked component city** in the coastal province of Pangasinan, **situated in the center of the province** ("Heart of Pangasinan").
- Area (per PSA 2013): **169.03 km²** = 3.10% of Pangasinan's total area. | `S1`, `S5`

### Coordinates & Elevation

- City center: **15.9277° N, 120.3478° E** (approx. 15°56′N, 120°21′E).
- Estimated elevation: **10.8 m** (35.4 ft) above mean sea level (PhilAtlas); Wikidata lists 12 m. | `S1`, `S6`

### Rivers and Water

- **Agno River** — major river of Pangasinan; the city's early poblacion sites were on the western/eastern banks of the Agno (historical). Wikipedia categorizes the city as a "Populated place on the Agno River".
- **San Juan River** — historically significant (site of poblacion transfer in 1718; legend of the church bell lost in the river).
- The city is **landlocked** (no marine waterbodies). | `S2`, `S1`, `S7`, `S8`

### Climate

Tropical climate with warm temperatures year-round and a pronounced wet season (May–October) and drier season (November–April). The Wikipedia weather box (source: World Weather Online) shows:

- Mean daily max: 30–33 °C; mean daily min: 21–24 °C.
- Wettest months: July–August (approx. 680–733 mm monthly precipitation in the cited data).
- Driest months: January (approx. 5 mm in the cited data). | `S2`, `S9`

**Verification note:** Figures above are from the Wikipedia-sourced World Weather Online data (accessed 2015) and should be treated as indicative. The official Philippine Atmospheric classification (Coronas type) for the city is **not yet confirmed** by an official PAGASA source in this research.

### Urban / Rural Barangay Split

- **86 barangays total**: 30 classified as urban (including Poblacion barangays along the boulevards/streets) and 56 as rural. | `S4`
