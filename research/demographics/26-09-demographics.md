# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Demographics and Statistics

## Scope

Census and statistical profile of San Carlos City, Pangasinan: 2020 census summary, historical census series, households, 2015 age–sex structure, voters and labor, language, and barangay-level highlights (full per-barangay figures live in `barangays/26-09-barangay-directory.md`).

## Summary

2020 census population **205,424**, up from 188,571 in 2015. Core census figures are PSA data via PhilAtlas (`S1`) with 2015 CBMS-based labor detail from the archived LGU demography page (`S2`); 2015-derived metrics are historical and the 2020 age/sex release is still pending.

### Registered Voters & Labor Data

- **Registered voters (2025 elections):** 126,193 (per Rappler's election summary, sourced from Comelec data). Earlier: 111,170 registered voters (2019, per PhilAtlas/Comelec: 53,617 male / 57,553 female). | `S3`, `S1`
- **2015 CBMS-based labor data (historical, official LGU demography page):** Of 113,194 persons aged 15+ (2015 CBMS city data), 49,847 were employed (43.8% of household population); 11,480 unemployed (unemployment rate 10.09%). Projected labor force by 2027: ~78,418. | `S2`

### Language

- **Primary language:** Pangasinan. The city is described as the "linguistic center of the Pangasinan language" (Wikipedia) and historically "Binalatongan" was a bastion of the Pangasinan (Caboloan) dialect.
- Secondary languages: Ilocano, Filipino, English. | `S4`

### Barangay-Level Population Statistics

Refer to `barangays/26-09-barangay-directory.md` for per-barangay 2020/2015 populations. Highlights:

- Largest barangay: **Turac** (6,919).
- Smallest barangay: **Mabini** (425).
- Fastest growth 2015–2020: **Bolosan** (+78.45%).
