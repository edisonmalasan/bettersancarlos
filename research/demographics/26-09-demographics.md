## Registered Voters & Labor Data

- **Registered voters (2025 elections):** 126,193 (per Rappler's election summary, sourced from Comelec data). Earlier: 111,170 registered voters (2019, per PhilAtlas/Comelec: 53,617 male / 57,553 female).
- **2015 CBMS-based labor data (historical, official LGU demography page):** Of 113,194 persons aged 15+ (2015 CBMS city data), 49,847 were employed (43.8% of household population); 11,480 unemployed (unemployment rate 10.09%). Projected labor force by 2027: ~78,418.
- Sources: [Rappler 2025 election summary](https://ph.rappler.com/elections/2025/local-race/pangasinan/san-carlos-city); [PhilAtlas](https://www.philatlas.com/luzon/r01/pangasinan/san-carlos.html); [LGU Demography page (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240603225002/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/demography)

## Language

- **Primary language:** Pangasinan. The city is described as the "linguistic center of the Pangasinan language" (Wikipedia) and historically "Binalatongan" was a bastion of the Pangasinan (Caboloan) dialect.
- Secondary languages: Ilocano, Filipino, English.
- Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos,_Pangasinan); Wikidata Q43165.
