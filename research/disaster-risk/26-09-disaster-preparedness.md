### Climate and Hazard Context

- The city sits on the **Agno River** system and low-lying plain (elevation ~10–12 m), making **flooding** a primary hazard (Agno floodwaters; sustained heavy monsoon/typhoon rainfall).
- **Typhoons:** Pangasinan is typhoon-prone; the city is inland but affected by seasonal typhoons (e.g., the region regularly experiences storm surges/flooding advisories — verify per-event).
- **Earthquakes:** Pangasinan is near the Philippine Fault System's regional strands; the city has participated in earthquake drills (old site news documented an earthquake drill at the BJMP compound, 2023). No local fault map verified in this research. | `S3`, `S6`
