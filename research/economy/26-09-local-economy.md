**Category:** economy

## Economic Profile

- San Carlos is an **agro-industrial city** — the "Mango and Bamboo Capital of the Philippines" — with major activities: mango production, bamboo craft/industry, livestock raising, crop production, inland fishing, pottery, food processing, tourism, commerce & trade, small-scale manufacturing, and flour-making.
- The city is positioned as an agro-industrial, education, and tourism center of Pangasinan (vision statement).
- Source: [Wikipedia — San Carlos, Pangasinan (Economy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Carlos,_Pangasinan); [Official LGU site (vision/mission)](https://sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/)

## Business Registration / Permits

- The city operates an **eBPLS** (electronic Business Permit and Licensing System) at https://prod4.ebpls.com/sancarlospangasinan/index.php — the official online business one-stop shop linked from the LGU website.
- The old official site published downloadable forms (business permit application, termination, peddlers permit, electrical/sanitary/occupancy permits) — historical reference.
