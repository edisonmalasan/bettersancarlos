# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Local Economy

## Scope

Economic profile of San Carlos City, Pangasinan: agro-industrial identity, income class, BLGF regular-income series, CMCI context, commercial centers, and business registration. Detailed CMCI figures live in `competitiveness/26-09-cmci-index.md`.

## Summary

Agro-industrial city ("Mango and Bamboo Capital"), 3rd class, with a verified BLGF income series through 2016; poverty incidence, current employment stats, and current CMCI scores remain unverified.

## Findings

### Economic Profile

- San Carlos is an **agro-industrial city** — the "Mango and Bamboo Capital of the Philippines" — with major activities: mango production, bamboo craft/industry, livestock raising, crop production, inland fishing, pottery, food processing, tourism, commerce & trade, small-scale manufacturing, and flour-making.
- The city is positioned as an agro-industrial, education, and tourism center of Pangasinan (vision statement). | `S1`, `S5`

### Income Class

- **3rd class city** (income classification). | `S7`, `S8`
