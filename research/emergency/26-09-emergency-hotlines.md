# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Emergency Hotlines and Public Safety

## Scope

National Philippines-wide emergency numbers plus city-level emergency contacts (current and historical) and related public-safety offices for San Carlos City, Pangasinan. Hospital emergency capacity details live in `health/26-09-health-facilities.md`.

## Summary

National hotlines (911, 117, 143, 8888) are widely documented (`S1`, `S2`); the DOH 1555 line is unconfirmed. City Hall trunk lines (075) 600-1432 and (075) 955-5929 are current on the live LGU Contact page (`S6`, `S7`). CDRRMO, police, and fire direct numbers still come only from the 2017 archive (`S4`) and must be re-verified before publication. A 2026-09-16 live re-check of the LGU Departments/Offices page (`S6`) confirms current office-holder names but publishes no station direct lines or station addresses; PRO1, BFP, and PNP portals were 403-blocked and DILG Region 1 carried no city-level contacts.

## Verification & Uncertainty

- National numbers are "widely documented national numbers": PNP, Red Cross, and 911 numbers are widely published by DILG/PNP, which are the canonical references. | `S1`, `S2`
- The DOH 1555 line should be confirmed with DOH.
- Current City Hall trunk lines were re-verified on the live LGU Contact page on 2026-09-16: City Hall Building, Poblacion, Palaris Street, San Carlos City, Pangasinan; Tel. Nos. (075) 955-5929 and (075) 600-1432; working hours Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. | `S6`
- Current office-holder names were re-verified on the live LGU Departments/Offices page on 2026-09-16: PLTCOL ELEAZAR C EDOLMO (Chief of Police, PNP San Carlos City), FCINSP RONALDO M PEREZ (Acting City Fire Marshall), BERNABE S. BACANI, JR. (City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Officer), JCINSP REYNALDO C OCRETO (City Jail Warden), DR. EDWIN DV. GUINTO (City Health Officer). The live page publishes names only — no station direct lines, no station addresses, no publication date. | `S6`
- CDRRMO/PNP/BFP direct lines remain historical 2017 values only (`S4`): (075) 955-5911, (075) 532-9896, (075) 544-2887. They were NOT re-verified as current and must not be published as current.
- 2017 city numbers are likely outdated: the city uses (075) 600-1432 as its main trunk line, and the CDRRMO/PNP/BFP offices must be re-dialed and re-verified before publishing on the live site.
- Ambulance / rescue hotlines (private services, City Health EMS) were not found in accessible official sources; needs manual verification.
- Traffic hotline not found; City Engineering / PNP traffic unit exists (per old site office list) but no separate hotline found.
- Addresses for the San Carlos PNP station and BFP station were not authoritatively captured; verify with DILG/PRO1.
