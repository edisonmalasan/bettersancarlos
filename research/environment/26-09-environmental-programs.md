# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Environmental Programs and Waste Management

## Scope

Environmental programs, waste management, and water resources of San Carlos City, Pangasinan, as documented in 2023 archived news and LGU menus. Waste-collection service details are not verified.

## Summary

Tree-planting, wildlife-day, and plastic-regulation engagements are documented from 2023 archived news; waste-management service details (collection schedules) and DENR compliance data are missing.

## Findings

### Environmental Programs

- **BFP San Carlos leads tree planting** (2023) — city fire station partnered with native tree-planting activities on the old official site news.
- **City observes World Wildlife Day** (2023) — documented event.
- The current official site tracks a **GAD Plan**, **DRRM Plan**, and a **List of PPAS** under its programs (see `infrastructure/26-09-city-projects.md`).
- The city historically engaged in **slope protection works** (an environmental/geo-hazard infrastructure project).
- **Coastal programs:** The city is **landlocked** (no marine coastline), so coastal programs do not apply; inland water (rivers/Agno) management is relevant. | `S1`, `S2`, `S4`

### City Environment / Sanitation Offices

- The city historically maintained a **General Services Office** and **City Cemetery Office** (see `government/26-09-government-directory.md`), which often handle sanitation/ecological solid waste in component cities. **Specific CLEÑA / CENRO office naming not verified.**
- A city news item from the old site documents **"regulation of plastic use"** engagement (2023) — the city monitors/regulates single-use plastics (enforcement program exists). | `S3`

### Water Resources / Rivers

- **Agno River** borders/context the city; **San Juan River** flows in the Poblacion area (see `geography`). These waterways need ecological status updates from DENR-EMB.
- No river cleanup program documented in this research.
