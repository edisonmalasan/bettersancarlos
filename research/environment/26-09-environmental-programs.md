# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Environmental Programs and Waste Management

**Category:** environment
**Verification status:** Historical/Partially Verified — environmental programs referenced from old official site news (2023) and LGU GAD/DRRM menus; waste-management service details (collection schedules) **not verified.**
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Environmental Programs (from official/archived sources)

- **BFP San Carlos leads tree planting** (2023) — city fire station partnered with native tree-planting activities on the old official site news.
- **City observes World Wildlife Day** (2023) — documented event.
- The current official site tracks a **GAD Plan**, **DRRM Plan**, and a **List of PPAS** under its programs (see `infrastructure/26-09-city-projects.md`).
- The city historically engaged in **slope protection works** (an environmental/geo-hazard infrastructure project).
- **Coastal programs:** The city is **landlocked** (no marine coastline), so coastal programs do not apply; inland water (rivers/Agno) management is relevant.

Sources: [Old official site — BFP tree planting (archived 2023)](https://web.archive.org/web/20230401225642/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/2023/03/15/bfp-san-carlos-leads-tree-planting/); [Old official site — World Wildlife Day (archived 2023)](https://web.archive.org/web/20230402001206/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/2023/03/10/city-observes-world-wildlife-day/); [Official LGU site](https://sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/)

- The city historically maintained a **General Services Office** and **City Cemetery Office** (see `government/26-09-government-directory.md`), which often handle sanitation/ecological solid waste in component cities. **Specific CLEÑA / CENRO office naming not verified.**
