# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Health Facilities (DOH-Licensed)

**Category:** health
**Verification status:** Partially Verified — hospital/facility names are corroborated by the official LGU "Evacuation routes and safe areas" page (archived 2017) and the Wikipedia city gallery. However, **DOH license numbers, bed capacities, and accreditation status were NOT obtainable** during research because the DOH HFSRB "List of Regulated Health Facilities" page and the OLRS portal were unreachable.
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Confirmed Facilities (names corroborated by official LGU sources)

The following facilities are named in the City Government of San Carlos's official evacuation-plan page (archived 2017-05-09) as high-rise buildings / safe shelters, confirming they operate in the city:

| Facility | Type | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Pangasinan Provincial Hospital (San Carlos)** | Government / Provincial Hospital | Named in official LGU evacuation plan + Wikipedia city gallery; operated by the Province of Pangasinan |
| **Virgen Milagrosa University Foundation (VMUF) Medical Center** | Private / University Hospital | Named in official LGU evacuation plan; VMUF is a major university in the city |
| **Blessed Family Hospital** | Private Hospital | Named in official LGU evacuation plan |
| **San Carlos Doctors Hospital** | Private Hospital | Named in Wikivoyage / general references as a high-rise building in the city |

## City Health Office

The **City Health Office (CHO)** operates under the San Carlos City government (confirmed in the 2024 archived LGU Departments/Offices directory). Contact number from the 2017 archived directory: **(075) 955-5917** — marked historical, re-verify.

## What Was NOT Confirmed (DOH License Data)

The following require DOH HFSRB verification and should NOT be published without it:
- DOH License to Operate numbers
- Bed capacity / authorized bed count
- Facility classification / level (Level 1 / 2 / 3)
- Accreditation status
- Services offered (e.g., PhilHealth accreditation, emergency services)
- License validity / expiration dates

## Research Attempts

1. **DOH HFSRB — List of Regulated Health Facilities** (https://hfsrb.doh.gov.ph/list-of-regulated-health-facilities/) — returned HTTP 404.
2. **DOH OLRS** (Online Licensing and Regulatory System, https://olrs.doh.gov.ph/) — accessible as a login portal but has no public facility search; OLRS implementation for Region I was deferred per Department Circular 2023-0375.
3. **HFSRB search by city** — no public parameterized search endpoint found.
4. **Direct web searches** for facility license numbers — no authoritative source returned results.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **File a request with DOH HFSRB** (Health Facilities and Services Regulatory Bureau) for the licensed facility list in San Carlos City, Pangasinan.
