# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Health Facilities

## Scope

Hospitals and health facilities in San Carlos City, Pangasinan: names corroborated from official LGU and encyclopedia sources, plus health-related services. DOH license data is tracked in `health/26-09-doh-facilities.md`.

## Summary

A core list of hospitals/facilities is documented, but DOH accreditation, addresses, and contact details for most facilities are not yet verified and are flagged for manual confirmation.

### Health-Related Services

The city's online forms historically included health-office-related permit forms (electrical, sanitary, occupancy permits — mostly under the Engineering/Health permit stream). See `transparency/26-09-full-disclosure.md` for the Citizen's Charter which lists the City Health Office among service offices.
