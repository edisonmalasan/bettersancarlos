# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Legislation Archive (Ordinances & Resolutions)

## Scope

Known legislation and governance documents of San Carlos City, Pangasinan, plus the status of digital legislation sources. Until a full archive is obtained, treat this file as a stub/status report, not a complete legal record.

## Summary

The council legislation subdomain was unreachable during research and most ordinance texts are unobtained; only a handful of historical items (RA 4487, RA 4866, one 2016 ordinance heading) are documented. Known items below are historical and must be re-verified.

## Verification & Uncertainty

- Some "RA 9184" materials are national (Government Procurement Policy Board) and relate to Bids and Awards Committee (BAC) operations, not local ordinances.
- The 2021 EO series on `sancarloscity.gov.ph` belongs to San Carlos City, Negros Occidental and must be excluded from Pangasinan material.
