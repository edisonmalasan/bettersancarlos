### 2024-08-22 — Fire Razed the Old Public Market

- The **old public market** of San Carlos City was **destroyed by fire** on **August 22, 2024**.
- Source: Philippine News Agency (PNA) article "Fire razes old public market in Pangasinan city" (per Wikipedia citation; PNA page returned 403 on direct fetch during this research). | `S1`
- **Note:** The city's public market has since moved/used the New Public Market (Binmaley–San Carlos Road). A rebuilt/redevelopment status should be confirmed.

### SGLG Recognition

- An official LGU article titled *"San Carlos City, Pangasinan: A Beacon of Excellence in Governance – Securing the SGLG Award"* confirms SGLG recognition. **Award year needs confirmation** (likely 2024/2025) from DILG. | `S5`

### CENPELCO Mass Disconnection (2024)

- CENPELCO announced a **mass disconnection** in the city starting **January 2024** for delinquent accounts under the Magna Carta for Electricity Consumers. | `S6`

### 2025 National and Local Elections (2025-05-12)

- Julier "Ayoy" Resuello re-elected Mayor; Joseres "Bogs" Resuello re-elected Vice Mayor; 10 councilors elected (see `government/26-09-city-officials.md`). | `S4`
