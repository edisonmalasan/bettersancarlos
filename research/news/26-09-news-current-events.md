# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — News and Current Events

> **Clarity:** Items are labeled **CURRENT** (recent/ongoing relevance as of 2026-09-04) vs **HISTORICAL** (no longer current, but meaningful context).

## Current / Significant Recent Developments

### 2024-08-22 — Fire razed the old public market (CURRENT context — recovery/rebuild ongoing)
- The **old public market** of San Carlos City was **destroyed by fire** on **August 22, 2024**.
- Source: [Philippine News Agency (PNA) — "Fire razes old public market in Pangasinan city"](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1231749) (per Wikipedia citation; PNA page returned 403 on direct fetch during this research).
- **Note:** The city's public market has since moved/used the New Public Market (Binmaley–San Carlos Road). A rebuilt/redevelopment status should be confirmed.

### SGLG (Seal of Good Local Governance) recognition (CURRENT)
- An official LGU article titled *"San Carlos City, Pangasinan: A Beacon of Excellence in Governance – Securing the SGLG Award"* confirms SGLG recognition. **Award year needs confirmation** (likely 2024/2025) from DILG.
- Source: [LGU official site (archived 2025-12-07)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251207010329/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/san-carlos-city-pangasinan-a-beacon-of-excellence-in-governance-securing-the-seal-of-good-local-governance-sglg-award)

### CENPELCO mass disconnection (2024) (CURRENT — payment/utility context)
- CENPELCO announced a **mass disconnection** in the city starting **January 2024** for delinquent accounts under the Magna Carta for Electricity Consumers.
- Source: [LGU official site (archived 2025-12-07)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251207004003/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/cenpelcos-implementation-of-mass-disconnection-in-san-carlos-pangasinan)

### 2025 National and Local Elections (2025-05-12) (CURRENT — new term)
- Julier "Ayoy" Resuello re-elected Mayor; Joseres "Bogs" Resuello re-elected Vice Mayor; 10 councilors elected (see `government/26-09-city-officials.md`).
- Source: [Rappler 2025](https://ph.rappler.com/elections/2025/local-race/pangasinan/san-carlos-city)
