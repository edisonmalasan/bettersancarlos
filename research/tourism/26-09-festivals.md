# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Festivals

**Category:** tourism
**Verification status:** Verified (official provincial + LGU sources) for the core Mango-Bamboo Festival facts; minor programming details are historical (2011 evidence).
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Mango-Bamboo Festival

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | **Mango-Bamboo Festival** |
| Launched | **2001** |
| Schedule | **Last week of April**, annually (often described as a week-long event) |
| Purpose | Promote San Carlos as the major trading center for **bamboo-based products/furniture** and top producer of **carabao mangoes** and other mango varieties |
| Origins | Launched by the city as an annual celebration to brand the "bamboo mango capital of the north" |

Sources: [Province of Pangasinan](https://www.pangasinan.gov.ph/city-municipalities/san-carlos-city/); [Old official site — Tourism (archived 2017)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170326022209/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/tourism/)

## Typical Festival Programming (historical — from archived LGU tourism page)

- **Float parade** — floats adorned with mango and bamboo decorations.
- **Street dancing competition** — barangay contingents compete.
- **Miss San Carlos City** — the city's prestige beauty pageant.
- **Culinary contests** — mango pie showcase, mango-based dishes.
- **Bamboo-craft competition**.
- Variety shows, entertainment, and **Laro ng Lahi** (native sports olympics).
- **Giant mango pie event** (world record bid on April 26, 2011).

Source: [LGU Tourism page (archived 2024)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240603225004/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/tourism); [Old official site — Facts and Figures (archived 2017)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170708092007/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/facts-and-figures/)
