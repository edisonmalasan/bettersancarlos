### Giant Mango Pie (Record)

- On **April 26, 2011**, the city displayed the **world's largest mango pie** (100 m², 2 in thick) at the city plaza as part of the Mango-Bamboo Festival; made with 2,000 kg mangoes by the residents of the 86 barangays under the San Carlos City Bakers Association. Total cost ₱400,000. | `S3`

### Festivals

See `tourism/26-09-festivals.md` — the **Mango-Bamboo Festival** (held in the last week of April) is the city's flagship event.
