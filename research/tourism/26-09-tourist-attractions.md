## Giant Mango Pie (Record)

- On **April 26, 2011**, the city displayed the **world's largest mango pie** (100 m², 2 in thick) at the city plaza as part of the Mango-Bamboo Festival; made with 2,000 kg mangoes by the residents of the 86 barangays under the San Carlos City Bakers Association. Total cost ₱400,000.
- Source: [Old official site — Facts and Figures (archived 2017)](https://web.archive.org/web/20170708092007/http://sancarloscitypangasinan.gov.ph/index.php/facts-and-figures/)
