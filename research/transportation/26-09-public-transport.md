# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Public Transport and Mobility

## Scope

How to reach San Carlos City and move within it: historical "Getting Here" data (2017 archive), carriers, capital access, intra-city modes, rail history, and traffic management. Routes, fares, and schedules are not verified.

## Summary

2017 archived guide gives entry points, travel times, and carriers; current operator schedules, intra-city routes/fares, and terminals are uncompiled. Do not publish unverified route maps.

## Findings

#### Entry Points (Historical)

- From **Manila**: via Camiling; via Bayambang; via Malasiqui.
- From **Manila (alternate)**: via Rosales → Bayambang → Malasiqui.
- From **Baguio City**: via San Fabian; via Dagupan. | `S1`

#### National Transport Carriers (Historical)

- **Five Star Bus Company**
- **First North Luzon Transit Inc.**
- **Pangasinan Solid North Transit Inc.** | `S1`

### Provincial Capital Access

- The city is **~19 km from Lingayen**, the provincial capital (road). | `S2`

### Intra-City Transport

- Typical Philippine provincial-city transport modes (jeepney, tricycle) exist per general knowledge, but **specific routes, terminals, and fares were not found in official sources** during this research. Do not publish unverified route maps.
- The city's boulevard-strip barangays (Rizal Ave, Perez Blvd, Quezon Blvd, Roxas Blvd) suggest the historic downtown layout.

### Rail (Historical)

- A barangay named **PNR Station Site (Poblacion)** and the **"PNR Site" (Poblacion)** indicates a former Philippine National Railways (PNR) station site in the city — this is a vestige of the historic Manila–Dagupan railway (the "Ferrocarril de Manila a Dagupan"), which closed in the 1980s. **Historical context only; no operational service.**

### Traffic Management

- No dedicated traffic hotline was found in official sources. The city was published a traffic division under the general services/PNP. See `emergency/26-09-emergency-hotlines.md` for the general city contact.
