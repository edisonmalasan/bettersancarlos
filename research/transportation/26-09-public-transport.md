## Intra-City Transport

- Typical Philippine provincial-city transport modes (jeepney, tricycle) exist per general knowledge, but **specific routes, terminals, and fares were not found in official sources** during this research. Do not publish unverified route maps.
- The city's boulevard-strip barangays (Rizal Ave, Perez Blvd, Quezon Blvd, Roxas Blvd) suggest the historic downtown layout.

## Rail (Historical)

- A barangay named **PNR Station Site (Poblacion)** and the **"PNR Site" (Poblacion)** indicates a former Philippine National Railways (PNR) station site in the city — this is a vestige of the historic Manila–Dagupan railway (the "Ferrocarril de Manila a Dagupan"), which closed in the 1980s. **Historical context only; no operational service.**
