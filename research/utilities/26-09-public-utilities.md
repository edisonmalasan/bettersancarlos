# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Public Utilities

**Category:** utilities
**Verification status:** Partially Verified — electricity provider (CENPELCO) confirmed by an official LGU-issued notice (archived); water/telco providers not authoritatively verified in this session.
**Research date:** 2026-09-04

## Electricity

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Distribution utility | **Central Pangasinan Electric Cooperative, Inc. (CENPELCO)** — the city's electric distribution cooperative. |
| Evidence | Official City Government notice titled **"CENPELCO's Implementation of Mass Disconnection in San Carlos, Pangasinan"** (announcing a mass disconnection activity starting January 2024 for delinquent accounts) — [LGU site (archived 2025-12-07)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251207004003/https://www.sancarlospangasinan.gov.ph/cenpelcos-implementation-of-mass-disconnection-in-san-carlos-pangasinan). Also, CENPELCO's own "franchise area" covers central Pangasinan including San Carlos (historical knowledge; confirm via NEA). |
| Consumers' rights | The disconnection notice references the **Magna Carta for Electricity Consumers** (48-hour grace after disconnection notice). |
| Power advisories | An archived "NGCP Power Advisory" news item appears on the LGU site (system/transmission advisories). |

**Customer service contact for CENPELCO was not verified in this research (office phone/email).** Obtain from CENPELCO/NEA.
