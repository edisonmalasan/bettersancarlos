# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Public Utilities

## Scope

Electricity, water, telecommunications, cable, sewage, and garbage collection for San Carlos City, Pangasinan. Only the electricity provider is confirmed; all provider contacts await verification.

## Summary

CENPELCO confirmed as the electricity distributor via an official LGU notice; water, telco, cable, and sewage providers are unverified or unresearched.

## Findings

### Electricity

| Item | Detail | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution utility | **Central Pangasinan Electric Cooperative, Inc. (CENPELCO)** — the city's electric distribution cooperative. | S1 |
| Evidence | Official City Government notice titled **"CENPELCO's Implementation of Mass Disconnection in San Carlos, Pangasinan"** (announcing a mass disconnection activity starting January 2024 for delinquent accounts) | S1 |
| Franchise context | CENPELCO's own "franchise area" covers central Pangasinan including San Carlos (historical knowledge; confirm via NEA) | S3 |
| Power advisories | An archived "NGCP Power Advisory" news item appears on the LGU site (system/transmission advisories) | S2 |

**Customer service contact for CENPELCO was not verified in this research (office phone/email).** Obtain from CENPELCO/NEA.

### Water

- **Provider confirmed: San Carlos City Water District (SCCWD)** — LWUA Conditional Certificate of Conformance awarded July 28, 1977 (SP Resolution No. 42, March 1977), per the district's official site verified live 2026-09-16 (`S5`); address Burgos–Padlan Street, San Carlos City, Pangasinan. District site content is 2017-vintage (8,461 connections as of 2015) and a 2014 PrimeWater joint venture leaves current operations unclear.
- **Do not publish SCCWD address, phones, emails, or leadership as verified** — re-verify currency first; the LWUA directory itself remains HTTP 403. See `utilities/26-09-water-district.md`.
- Management as published (2017-vintage, currency unconfirmed): General Manager Mr. Jose Jovares A. Resuello II with a five-member board; October 2014 joint venture with Primewater Infrastructure Corporation — current customer-service operator NOT established. | `S5`
