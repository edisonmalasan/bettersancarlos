# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Water District

## Scope

Determine the authoritative potable-water service provider for San Carlos City, Pangasinan.

## Summary

Provider identity is confirmed: the **San Carlos City Water District (SCCWD)** serves the city under LWUA Conditional Certificate of Conformance awarded July 28, 1977 (established by Sangguniang Panlungsod Resolution No. 42, March 1977), per its official site verified live on 2026-09-16 (`S4`, `S5`, `S6`). Management as published: General Manager Mr. Jose Jovares A. Resuello II with a five-member Board of Directors (2017-vintage page — currency unconfirmed). Office address, phone numbers, and emails appear on the district's 2017-vintage site and need re-verification before any canonical use; service-area zones and pumping-station inventory are documented as of Dec 31, 2015. The LWUA directory itself remains HTTP 403.
