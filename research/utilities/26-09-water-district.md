# San Carlos City, Pangasinan — Water District

## Scope

Determine the authoritative potable-water service provider for San Carlos City, Pangasinan.

## Summary

Provider identity is confirmed: the **San Carlos City Water District (SCCWD)** serves the city under LWUA Conditional Certificate of Conformance awarded July 28, 1977 (established by Sangguniang Panlungsod Resolution No. 42, March 1977), per its official site verified live on 2026-09-16 (`S4`, `S5`, `S6`). Office address, phone numbers, and emails appear on the district's 2017-vintage site and need re-verification before any canonical use; service-area currency, rates, and the PrimeWater joint-venture operating role need confirmation. The LWUA directory itself remains HTTP 403.

## Research Question

Which entity authoritatively provides potable water to San Carlos City, Pangasinan, and what are its office, contacts, service area, and rates?

## Current Conclusion

Provider identity confirmed as San Carlos City Water District (LWUA CCC July 28, 1977). Do not publish its address, phones, emails, leadership, service area, or rates as verified: the district site content is 2017-vintage (8,461 connections as of Dec 31, 2015) and the 2014 PrimeWater joint venture leaves the current operating arrangement unclear. Re-verify contacts and operations before any canonical use.

## Context

San Carlos City, being a 3rd-class component city with a 2025 population of 205,424, requires a potable water supply system. Philippine cities of this size are typically served by either:

- A **Water District** (a local government-owned and controlled corporation under LWUA), or
- A **City-managed water system** (directly operated by the LGU), or
- A **private water service provider**
